Welcome to the Beautiful Tropical Oasis, Englewood Beach. We hope that our little piece of paradise will help you find your paradise.

May the salt air slow your steps, the turquoise waters quiet your mind, and this cozy cottage hold every soft moment you came looking for.

From sunrise coffee on the patio to sandy toes and golden evenings under the palms — we’re so glad you’re here. Make yourself at home. Leave only footprints, and take home a little more peace than you arrived with.

About the cottage

Open living, coastal light, room to gather

Tropical Oasis, Englewood Beach is designed for easy days and unhurried evenings — an open floor plan that flows from kitchen to dining to outdoor living, all just a short stroll from soft sand and turquoise water.

Open kitchen & island

Cook together around a generous island with granite counters, white cabinetry, and stainless appliances. The open layout keeps everyone connected while meals come together.

Open living & dining

A bright great room for family meals, game nights, and long conversations after a day at the beach — living, dining, and kitchen all in one easy flow.

Outdoor living

Settle into the chairs under the palms just outside the turquoise cottage — easy outdoor time after the beach, with a fully fenced yard for downtime.

Rest easy after the beach

Two comfortable bedrooms and two bathrooms mean space to unwind. Soft linens, warm wood, and quiet coastal light turn the cottage into your own private oasis after salt, sun, and sand.

  • Cozy bedrooms with coastal comfort
  • Two full bathrooms for easy mornings
  • Open living for groups and families
